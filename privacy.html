Privacy policy

rclone (covermem) is used only by its owner, to copy the owner's own files from the owner's Google Cloud Storage bucket to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It reads and writes only the owner's own Google Drive files.
  • It collects no personal data and keeps no data beyond the files it copies.
  • It shares nothing with anyone, and no other person uses it.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
